Our Story

Committed to Excellence Since 2005

LifeCare Clinic was founded in 2005 with a simple yet powerful vision: to provide world-class healthcare that is accessible, affordable, and compassionate. Over the years, we have grown into one of the most trusted healthcare providers in the region.

Our team of over 24 specialist doctors, 100+ support staff, and modern infrastructure ensures that every patient receives the best possible care. We combine advanced medical technology with a human touch.
